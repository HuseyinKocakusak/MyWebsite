Academia

I am a PhD student at the University of Vienna, in the Vienna Doctoral School of Pharmaceutical, Nutritional and Sport Sciences (PhaNuSpo). I investigate the roles of extracellular vesicles, and the proteins and sncRNAs they carry, in communication between neurons. In my work, I use human iPSC-derived neurons and mouse primary cortical neurons.

Activity-dependent release of 5′tRF GluCTC modulates neuronal molecular networks in a human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived neuronal model (2026)

Research (in vitro). Cell Death & Disease. In human iPSC-derived cortical neurons, glutamate/picrotoxin-induced hyperexcitability raised intracellular and extracellular 5′tRF GluCTC, whose production depended on DICER and Angiogenin; a 5′tRF GluCTC mimic downregulated proteins of RNA-protein complexes, translation and neuronal signaling (including the epilepsy-associated TUBB2B, EEF1A1/2 and EIF4A2) and increased spike and burst frequency at 24 h.

A gut microbial odd-chain fatty acid alleviates atherosclerosis in mice (2026)

Research (in vivo, human data). Nature. Bacteroides uniformis, the most abundant health-associated species in gut metagenomes of 223 ASCVD patients vs 189 controls, alleviated atherosclerosis in Apoe-/- mice by upregulating hepatic LDL receptor; its odd-chain fatty acid pentadecanoic acid (C15:0) reduced plaque burden by about 50% and improved plasma lipids by directly inhibiting HMG-CoA reductase, and was markedly depleted in patients with dyslipidaemia.

Astrocyte-derived extracellular vesicles regulate proopiomelanocortin neurons and modify food intake and glucose metabolism (2026)

Research (in vitro, in vivo). Metabolism. EVs from hypothalamic astrocytes were internalized by POMC neurons and increased Pomc expression; infused into the lateral ventricle or arcuate nucleus of male mice, they altered mitochondria and mitochondria-ER contacts in POMC neurons, food intake, eating patterns and glucose metabolism, with effects and EV miRNA content depending on prior astrocyte exposure to palmitic or oleic acid and differing between male- and female-derived astrocytes.

The Cytoplasmic MicroRNA-Production Complex Regulates MicroRNA Sorting to Extracellular Vesicles During Synaptogenesis (2026)

Research (in vitro). Advanced Science. Shows that TRBP, a core accessory protein of the cytoplasmic miRNA-production machinery, interacts with EV RNA-binding proteins and regulates miRNA secretion in small EVs; TRBP-regulated EV-miRNAs are enriched in a Syncrip-recognition motif, Syncrip binds TRBP at the endoplasmic reticulum, and ER-endosome contact sites promote Syncrip loading to endosomes; Syncrip mutants altered synapse formation in EV-donor and recipient neurons.

Extracellular Vesicles Derived from M2 Microglia and Enriched in miR-27b-3p Attenuate Mitochondria-Dependent Endothelial Apoptosis via the MKK4/JNK Pathway and Alleviate BBB Disruption After Intracerebral Hemorrhage (2026)

Extracellular exosomal RNAs are glyco-modified (2025)

Research (in vitro). Nature Cell Biology. Metabolic labeling with an Ac4GalNAz probe reveals small, non-coding, glyco-modified RNAs (glycoRNAs) that are RNase-resistant and carried mainly as intraluminal exosome cargo, distinct from cell-surface glycoRNAs; exosomal glycoRNAs transfer to naive cells, and blocking glycan transfer to proteins reduces their sorting into exosomes.

Distinct small non-coding RNA landscape in the axons and released extracellular vesicles of developing primary cortical neurons and the axoplasm of adult nerves (2021)

Research (in vitro, in vivo). RNA Biology. Sequencing of axons and extracellular vesicles of developing cortical neurons and of adult nerve axoplasm reveals small non-coding RNAs well beyond miRNAs, including fragments of tRNAs, sno/snRNAs, Y RNAs and vtRNAs; miRNAs dominate whole cells, but tRNA fragments (mainly from Gly-GCC, Val-CAC and Val-AAC) predominate in axons and EVs, with a shift from 5′ to 3′ tRNA halves in mature axons in vivo.

About Me

Outside the lab, I apply the same discipline to training, combining strength work, endurance running, and evidence-based nutrition to continuously improve physical performance. I also practice beekeeping with a scientific mindset, emphasizing natural production, sustainability, and traceable quality.

I value rigorous thinking, consistent work, and projects that create measurable impact in research, sport, and the products I develop.
